# Week 9 procedure — classes and objects

1. Model one workout record with `WorkoutSession`.
2. Model a learner with `Member`, keeping its state private.
3. Add sessions through a `log` behaviour instead of editing the array directly.
4. Calculate total and average minutes through member methods.
5. Print an object-driven progress report.

Compile and run with `javac FitnessTrackerApp.java` followed by `java FitnessTrackerApp`.
