# Week 10 procedure — validation and exceptions

1. Convert the submitted workout duration to an integer inside a guarded method.
2. Reject values outside the one-to-300 minute range.
3. Convert non-numeric input into a learner-friendly checked exception.
4. Process valid and invalid sample submissions without stopping the programme.

Compile and run with `javac FitnessInputValidator.java` followed by `java FitnessInputValidator`.
