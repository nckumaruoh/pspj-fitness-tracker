# Week 12 procedure — final collections and streams analytics

1. Create a list of workout-session objects for three members.
2. Group and total minutes by member using a stream collector.
3. Find the top member from the generated map.
4. Filter members who meet the 90-minute weekly goal.
5. Print the final analytics release report.

Compile and run with `javac FitnessAnalytics.java` followed by `java FitnessAnalytics`.
